claudio, the voice in front of your coding-agent sessions: talk, and your bb threads work.

## What you get

- A **claudio** tab in every thread's right panel: the conversation as you speak, tables and code drawn as in a
  thread, and a composer to type, attach files, run claudio's commands, and pick its model and reasoning.
- Your threads by voice: ask what they are doing, hand them a task, start a new one. claudio asks you before
  every send.
- When a thread you handed work to finishes, claudio tells you what it did.
- A thread's permission prompt or question is read out as soon as bb records it; answer by voice and claudio
  answers it in bb.

## How it works

The plugin starts claudio (a Python program, installed separately) in the background and shows what it says and
does. Clicking its avatar starts it, or restarts it; it never restarts alone, after `/exit` or a crash. Run one
claudio at a time: one in a terminal too would fight over the mic.
