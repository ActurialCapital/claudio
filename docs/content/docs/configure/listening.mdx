The defaults suit one person talking at a desk. Change them when it cuts you off, waits too long, or hears the wrong people.

## When your turn ends

Deepgram Flux gives every moment of your speech a confidence that you have finished. Three settings act on it:

<Settings only={['eager_eot', 'eot', 'eot_timeout_ms']} />

| If | Try |
|---|---|
| It answers before you have finished a thought | raise `eot` (0.8) and `eot_timeout_ms` (2500) |
| It waits too long after you stop | lower `eot` (0.6) or `eot_timeout_ms` (1000) |
| Replies feel slow to start | lower `eager_eot` so the brain starts earlier |
| Early starts are often wasted and you see `· resumed` | raise `eager_eot` closer to `eot` |

An early start costs nothing you hear: its audio is held until your turn is final and the words match. It only spends brain time.

Changes reconnect the ears straight away, so you can tune by voice while you talk: "set eot to 0.8".

## Other languages

```bash
claudio set ears flux-general-multi
```

The persona is in English. Edit [`prompt.md`](/docs/configure/persona) to have it answer in your language, and pick a voice that speaks it.

## Interrupting

<Settings only={['barge_in']} />

With `barge_in` on, talking over a reply pauses it, then cuts it or lets it go on. [Talking over it](/docs/guide/talking#talking-over-it) explains the rules. On speakers this needs the echo canceller, which is shown as `echo cancel on` in the banner.

Turn it off if a noisy room keeps pausing it. Replies then play through, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> cuts them.

## Push to talk

<Settings only={['push_to_talk']} />

The mic stays closed until you press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, then closes again when your turn ends. The state line reads `push to talk · Enter opens the mic` while it is closed.

## Rooms with other people

<Settings only={['address_me']} />

Only turns that start with "claudio" count. Everything else is shown dim and ignored. "claudio, what's the sequencer doing?" works; the name is dropped before the brain sees the turn.
